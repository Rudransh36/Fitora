Ayurvedic Sports Nutrition & Digestive Balance

🌿 Ahara & Agni: The Vedic Science of Athletic Vitality

Ayurveda views nutrition through Agni (digestive fire) and Ojas (vital energy and immunity). For competitive athletes, balancing macronutrients with digestive stamina ensures complete nutrient assimilation, reduced inflammation, and faster recovery without metabolic sluggishness.

Tridosha Optimization for Multi-Sport Athletes

💨 Vata (Movement & Speed)
Badminton & Quick Reflexes

High movement depletes lubrication and causes joint stiffness.

Key Foods: Ghee, warm spiced milk, soaked almonds, dates, sesame seeds.
🔥 Pitta (Metabolism & Fire)
Cricket Under Sun & High Power

Excessive heat causes acidity, muscle cramping, and inflammation.

Key Foods: Fresh Masala Taak (buttermilk), coconut water, mint, soaked chia seeds.
⛰️ Kapha (Structure & Strength)
Gym Hypertrophy & Heavy Lifts

Provides foundational skeletal mass, but excess leads to lethargy.

Key Foods: Jowar/Bajra Bhakri, sprouted matki usal, ginger, black pepper.

The 6 Tastes (Shad Rasa) for Athletic Meal Completeness

1. Madhura (Sweet): Whole grains, milk, rice (builds muscle mass)
2. Amla (Sour): Taak, lemon, amla (promotes enzyme secretion)
3. Lavana (Salty): Rock salt / Sendha Namak (restores electrolytes)
4. Katu (Pungent): Ginger, black pepper (boosts metabolism & Agni)
5. Tikta (Bitter): Methi greens, turmeric (detoxifies & cleanses blood)
6. Kashaya (Astringent): Sprouted lentils, triphala (promotes tissue recovery)
